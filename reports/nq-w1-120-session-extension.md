# NQ W1 120-session extension candidate

**Prepared:** 2026-10-14. **Disposition:** The June 2–November 21, 2022 interval is a **structurally feasible 120-CME-trade-date research candidate**, subject to unresolved calendar, missing-minute, and causal-volume evidence. It is **not yet a valid 120-session A/B run** for historical HAR fitting. No paid data was retrieved; source OHLCV, certification tiers, production estimators, and historical HAR artifacts were preserved.

The machine-readable [candidate](../results/nq-w1-120-session-candidate.json) records every date, selected contract, predecessor volume ranking, active segment, minute-grid result, exact proposed request, account quote, and potential HAR target/origin pair. It is generated by the [offline builder](../scripts/build_nq_w1_120_candidate.py); the [metadata-only quote script](../scripts/quote_nq_w1_120_gaps.py) adds current account estimates. Source SHA-256 values in the JSON bind the retained Parquet and Phase 3 decision ledger. The [prior holiday remapping validation](nq-w1-holiday-remapping-validation.md) and [original CME workbook review](nq-2022-cme-holiday-evidence.md) are the calendar basis.

## Calendar count and predecessor boundary

I independently enumerated civil weekdays from **June 2 through November 21** inclusive: **123**. The preserved CME Juneteenth, Independence Day, and Labor Day workbooks assign each Sunday-through-Tuesday holiday session to the **Tuesday** Globex trade date, eliminating June 20, July 4, and September 5 as separate trade-date identities. This yields **120 distinct ordered candidate trade dates**, starting June 2 and ending November 21. Every one of the 123 weekday labels is present in the retained decision ledger; no intervening weekday is silently skipped or classified as an unknown closure. The three documented holiday dates have two active segments each, 2,520 minutes, with the five-hour Monday halt/pre-open excluded. Ordinary candidate dates have one 1,380-minute segment. This establishes the *structural count under the documented calendar rule*. It does not prove every remaining date's official exception boundary: the nine guarded dates listed below still need dated NQ schedule review.

June 1 is the causal predecessor for the new first date, June 2. Its selected **NQM22** bar grid has **1,380/1,380** unique active minutes, no off-grid selected bar, and no duplicate. Ranking the contracts present on June 1 by retained **May 31** volume gives NQM22 **694,323**, NQU22 **1,225**, and NQZ22 **26**; NQM22 wins. Its June 1 retained volume **672,372** then beats NQU22 **1,922** and NQZ22 **15** for June 2. June 2's NQM22 volume **615,114** determines June 3; June 3's **641,540** determines June 6; June 6's **531,252** determines June 7. The chain is in the JSON for all 120 dates, with each day's candidate universe ranked only on the preceding completed **corrected CME trade date**. All 120 winners match the historical selected contracts; roll changes remain **June 14 NQU22** and **September 13 NQZ22**. The earlier 117 corrected W1 dates were independently reconciled with the previous candidate for predecessor, winner, volume ranking, and grid result. These are retained-panel calculations, not proof that thin-contract exchange volume or the May 31 boundary is complete.

The June 1 ledger decision remains **Tier C, holiday/early-close unverified**, because its May 31 predecessor lies in the Memorial Day guard chain. June 2 remains historical **Tier C, causal predecessor incomplete**. The retained June 1 bars resolve its ordinary *timestamp coverage* only; they do not validate the May 30–June 1 official trade-date mapping or source-volume completeness. June 3 remains historical B. June 6 is historical C because of its isolated minute hole, and June 7 is historical C because it depends on June 6. No tier was changed.

## HAR upper bound and preregistered split

With the corrected 120-date index, the first **22 sessions (June 2–July 5)** are feature warmup. The maximum is **98 next-session target rows**: 50 train targets **July 6–September 14** (ordinals 23–72), 20 validation targets **September 15–October 12** (73–92), and 28 untouched test targets **October 13–November 21** (93–120). The first forecast uses features through the July 5 CME trade date at its **July 5 21:00 UTC** session end, before the July 6 session opens at **22:00 UTC**. The last uses features through November 18 at **November 18 22:00 UTC**, before the November 21 trade date opens Sunday **November 20 23:00 UTC**. The JSON enumerates all 98 origins, 5/22-session feature starts, target sessions, and nominal target completion times; each origin precedes its target's active start, and each target finishes after its origin.

These are **potential**, not usable, HAR rows. At the nominal session-end boundary each preceding RV could first be known, but historical vendor publication/revision latency is not established. A target RV is available only after its own final required bar; fits at an origin may use only prior targets available by that origin. With the five present gaps and no imputation, **65 of the 98 nominal target rows** have a missing-minute date in their target or 22-session feature window (38 train, 4 validation, 23 test). More decisively, the preregistered gate requires all 120 consecutive dates to be accepted; it cannot be met by dropping those rows. This follows the frozen [exploratory HAR study design](nq-har-exploratory-study-design.md), and no historical RV or HAR forecast was fitted or scored.

## Remaining blockers and feasibility

1. **Official exception-date proof.** The three holiday Tuesday trade dates are supported by original CME workbooks, but the following Wednesdays **June 22, July 6, September 7** and the guarded groups **October 10–12, November 9–11** still lack dated NQ-specific schedule confirmation. Their retained bars fill the ordinary candidate grid, which does not certify the official trade-date boundary. The June 1 / May 31 Memorial Day boundary also remains provisional for the first selection chain. There is no unobserved intervening weekday, but these exception reviews prevent a claim that all 120 are independently certified official trade dates.
2. **Five active-minute evidence holes.** June 6, July 27, August 16, August 18, and October 13 need the bounded `trades`/`status` evidence above and, if necessary, provider sequence/completeness follow-up. The five gaps also affect downstream causal selection on **June 7, July 28, August 17, August 19, and October 14**. Zero filling or silently dropping a date is not allowed by the study design.
3. **Causal and eligibility propagation.** The retained-panel winner replay is complete for the corrected index, but predecessor date validity and complete volume for every current candidate contract are not independently proved. The historical ledger still marks **93 B and 27 C** of these 120 identities; old labels were retained solely for comparison and were not recertified. Calendar reassignment, segment RV/BPV and 5-minute RV support, 5/22-date features, target availability, and any downstream fit must be rebuilt and reviewed under a new version only after the evidence gates clear.

**Decision:** The date count, grid construction, and prespecified 50/20/28 allocation are structurally feasible. The candidate is currently **blocked for a valid 120-session research sample and historical HAR fit** by the official-calendar exceptions, five minute gaps, and causal/eligibility evidence above. The proposed five-gap retrieval estimate is **$0.000777244568**; any paid retrieval requires new explicit authorization.
