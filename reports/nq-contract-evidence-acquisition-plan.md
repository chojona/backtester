# NQ contract evidence acquisition plan

**Date:** 2026-10-14 (America/New_York)
**Scope:** the 120 target CME trade dates from June 2 through November 21, 2022, and their completed predecessor dates from June 1 through November 18. The combined May 31 session is included as an optional boundary check for June 1.
**Status:** discovery and metadata quotes only. No paid time-series request was made. The exact date-level coverage and request objects are in [`nq-contract-evidence-acquisition-plan.json`](../results/nq-contract-evidence-acquisition-plan.json).

## Decision first

**Do not acquire a broad trade tape now.** The cheapest quoted first step is a complete-period Databento `definition` plus `statistics` request for the 11 raw NQ symbols appearing in the changing nine-contract rosters, estimated at **$0.0290** for this account. It is a *conditional diagnostic*, not yet sufficient evidence to use the selection at the existing forecast origin. Confirm first whether the historical CME Daily Bulletin or equivalent exchange contract-month report is accessible and contains all nine NQ rows, explicit zero-volume rows, CME trade-date labels, and source release times. Its historical price and 2022 contract-level content could not be verified from the public catalog/index, so the cost is **unknown**, not assumed free.

The central feasibility issue is timing. The intended forecast origin is the prior CME trade-date close, normally **5 p.m. ET**; the next session normally begins **6 p.m. ET**. CME says the [preliminary Daily Bulletin updates around midnight CT and the final around 10 a.m. CT the following business day](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/daily-bulletin.html). The [CME VOI page](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/volume-open-interest.html) calls end-of-day VOI preliminary and says official data come the next morning. Those reports can validate historical rankings **after the fact**, but their final totals cannot be treated as available at the prior-close origin. A causal ranking at that origin needs timestamped, as-received volume messages or trade aggregation with feed-completeness and correction evidence; otherwise the experiment must preregister a later origin and a compatible target window.

## What the retained data already cover

The preceding [causal universe audit](nq-causal-contract-universe-audit.md) reconstructed CME's [2022 nine-contract listing cycle](https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/ser/2022/04/SER-8975.pdf). Each target has five nearby quarters plus four additional December expiries. Across 120 dates there are **1,080 eligible target-contract/predecessor-volume cells**: **328** have positive retained one-minute OHLCV volume and **752** have no retained predecessor volume. Every date has six or seven missing-volume cells; no full-roster winner is verified. There are **zero confirmed zero-volume cells**. Two of the 752 are contracts that the rule implies were newly added between the predecessor and target (`NQU23` for June 21 and `NQZ27` for September 19); a dated venue definition snapshot must confirm their prior-day nonlisting before assigning structural zero.

The local acquisition requested only three nearby raw symbols per chunk. Its retained definitions cover those requested symbols, not the full nine-contract book. A `volume=0` assigned when a requested contract lacks a predecessor bar is also unproven zero. The existing positive OHLCV sums are lower bounds unless the underlying trade feed is independently complete. The JSON lists all nine expected contracts, every observed positive prior volume, every missing cell, origin cutoff, and the possible CME report path for **each of 120 target dates**.

The predecessor sessions on **June 21, July 5, and September 6** must combine the Sunday-to-Monday and Monday-to-Tuesday active intervals under one Tuesday CME trade date, as supported by the locally preserved CME holiday workbooks. The optional **May 31** check has the same combined-session structure. For any candidate source, compare its CME trade-date label or `ts_ref` to those workbooks before comparing totals. Do not compare a UTC-calendar-day `ohlcv-1d` record directly with a research trade-date sum. [Databento documents that its `ohlcv-1d` bars use UTC dates, omit intervals with no trade, and can differ from official venue volume](https://databento.com/docs/knowledge-base).

## Minimal staged evidence package

1. **Free inspection before any purchase.** Preserve the CME May 23 listing notice and the four local holiday workbooks. Obtain one June-roll and one September-roll historical Daily Bulletin sample, ideally June 13 and September 12, and check whether every expected NQ outright has a row or explicit zero, how Globex/ClearPort/block volume is defined, whether trade dates match the corrected Tuesday sessions, and when each revision was published. If the public archive does not provide it, request an exact DataMine catalog quote. The publicly indexed `daily_volume_YYYYMMDD.xlsx` filenames are candidate files only; their contents were not verified here.
2. **Smallest quoted provider diagnostic, only after separate paid authorization.** Request `GLBX.MDP3` `definition` for **May 23–November 22 UTC** and `statistics` for **May 29–November 20 UTC**, explicit `raw_symbol` list `NQM2, NQU2, NQZ2, NQH3, NQM3, NQU3, NQZ3, NQZ4, NQZ5, NQZ6, NQZ7`. Estimated combined cost **$0.029027152806**. Resolve actual instrument IDs and activation/expiration histories, `CLEARED_VOLUME` records, `ts_ref`, release/revision times, true zero representation, and all 120 corrected predecessor dates. Do not infer zero from no record.
3. **Only if needed for the original Globex rule.** Add `ohlcv-1h` and `status` for the same symbols, **May 29–November 19 UTC**; estimated incremental **$0.061874985695**, cumulative **$0.090902138501** with definitions/statistics. Aggregate each CME active interval, including combined holiday parts, and reconcile against locally retained 1m totals and independent official records. A mismatch can reflect clearing-only activity, bar construction, corrections, or source loss; classify it before ranking.
4. **Trade fallback, exact bounded scope after discrepancy review.** Request `trades` only for cells whose rank remains consequential and whose volume/zero cannot be certified, using the dated active UTC intervals, explicit raw symbol and a small boundary buffer if publication/correction records require one. Obtain new metadata quotes for those exact requests. The **$69.12** broad-range quote is a comparison ceiling for an unbounded approach, not a proposed order or a cost cap.

A cell is resolved only by an authoritative numeric prior trade-date volume with matching metric and as-of time, an explicit authoritative zero, or a certified upper bound below the known leader; rule-confirmed nonlisting may supply structural zero. A date is resolved only when every eligible candidate is resolved and the entire roster is verified. Stop if the official source uses a different volume scope without an explicit preregistered choice of ranking metric. Preserve the existing `max(volume, contract code)` tie rule.
