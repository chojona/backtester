## What was frozen

The generator is [`scripts/build_nq_w1_final_research_dataset.py`](../scripts/build_nq_w1_final_research_dataset.py). Reproduction requires the retained Git-ignored raw DBNs as well as the tracked code. The outputs are content-addressed snapshots; changing a source requires a new version and manifest, not editing these decision records in place.

[`daily-estimators-and-decisions.json`](../results/nq-w1-final-research-dataset/daily-estimators-and-decisions.json) gives every candidate contract, known volume rank, unresolved competitor, UTC active segments, minute counts, conditional volatility values, uncertainty flags, unchanged historical tier, and four separate chronology times. [`minute-classifications.csv.gz`](../results/nq-w1-final-research-dataset/minute-classifications.csv.gz) contains one row for every expected active minute, its observed-trade or evidenced provider-no-trade class, price/return, and evidence hash for the five exceptions. These values are **conditional** on the candidate contract and calendar, including unresolved dates; they are not approved HAR targets.

The three in-sample Tuesday sessions each have two active UTC intervals totaling 2,520 minutes, with the Monday halt excluded. The prior [calendar review](nq-w1-final-calendar-readiness.md) details workbook cells, hashes, UTC conversions, and the May 31 → June 1 predecessor boundary.

The following **nine dates remain provisional**: June 22, July 6, September 7, October 10–12, and November 9–11. The Wednesday sessions inherit the ordinary rule after the dated holiday Tuesday closes. The October and November guard windows also have complete ordinary-rule observed grids. Neither fact independently establishes that no dated NQ exception or changed trade-date assignment applied. A targeted search of CME's public material did not yield a dated 2022 NQ Globex schedule or complete effective exception register for these nine; clearing and settlement advisories do not specify NQ session hours. The output preserves ordinary-rule candidate boundaries and the `provisional` flag. Exact authoritative NQ hours and trade-date evidence, with a retained source and hash, are needed before clearing them. There are 123 civil weekdays in the interval; the three documented combined Tuesday dates remove separate June 20, July 4, and September 5 trade-date identities, leaving 120 candidates with no silently omitted intervening weekday.

## 3. Clock policy and volatility checks

The draft [**`nq-w1-exploratory-clock-and-availability-v1.1.0-draft`** policy](../results/nq-w1-final-research-dataset/exploratory-policy-v1.1.0.md) permits a previous observed trade price to be carried for exactly five preserved, authenticated provider no-trade minutes: June 6, July 27, August 16, August 18, and October 13. Each bounded trades DBN returned zero records, and its paired session-open-through-gap status response reported trading/quoting active. The generator reopens and verifies all ten DBNs and JSONL hashes, metadata, symbol and UTC bounds through the existing evidence validator. The gap minute has return zero; the next observed bar keeps its genuine move. No trade, bar, or volume is invented. This is provider-stream clock treatment, subject to upstream completeness; it does not revise the original HAR preregistration or historical certification. Any other absent active minute is `UNKNOWN` and invalidates the complete-window estimator.

The replay classifies **169,015 observed trade minutes + five evidenced provider-no-trade minutes = 169,020 expected active minutes**. It uses the frozen v2 return reference: previous close across research-label boundaries when active price continuity holds; first bar open at active interval start; no price bridge over a scheduled halt. RV5 and RV15 sum squared returns of nonoverlapping, complete blocks anchored at each Overnight/RTH/Post-RTH research-segment opening. BPV uses uncorrected adjacent absolute-return products only within a research segment, multiplied by π/2. There are **33,804 five-minute** and **11,268 fifteen-minute** complete blocks, each covering all 169,020 expected minutes. Each date has `RV1 = RS+ + RS−` within `1e−15`, finite nonnegative estimators, and matched RV1/RV5/RV15 coverage. No cross-segment BPV pair is added. All 120 RV1/RV5/RS/BPV results agree with the already independently checked [v2 clock replay](nq-clock-returns-v2-validation.md); RV15 is added here under the same segment anchoring and coverage rule. The arithmetic passes **conditionally** on the selected path and provisional calendar.

## 4. Availability, decisions, and one-session lag

For each date, the ledger separately records predecessor **observation cutoff** at its active close, **assumed data finalization** 45 minutes later, **contract-selection decision** at that same deadline, and **earliest executable target session** at its first active instant. In ordinary EDT these are 17:00, 17:45, 17:45, and 18:00 New York time; Friday decisions precede Sunday's reopening. The selected contract uses only the completed predecessor date's candidate electronic volumes. A target opening bar, target volume, or target RV never enters its own selection. Completed feature and target RV would become available only after their respective finalization deadlines. These are retrospective assumptions because the original OHLCV publication/revision timestamps are not retained; they do not establish strict point-in-time causality. The guard dates would need timing re-evaluation if authoritative hours differ.

Under a **one-completed-session availability lag**, predecessor final volumes arrive after the current target begins. All 120 frozen predecessor decisions fail the proposed pre-open deadline; choosing an older predecessor would define a different selector and is not used here. The fixed 22/50/20/28 chronology remains intact, with zero released rows. The 98 positional targets are only potential rows; neither missing selector dates nor calendar guards are dropped, joined around, or refitted.

## Release gate and exact blockers

**NO-GO; research-ready = false.** Mathematical and matched-sampling checks pass for the candidate clock path, but every date still lacks an explicitly permitted *complete* treatment under the unchanged 120-session gate. The blocking evidence is:

1. Ten unknown predecessor electronic-volume cells have no independent exact total or valid upper bound, so their front-three winners cannot be established without an exploratory winner assumption.
2. Nine calendar-guard dates lack authoritative dated NQ hours/trade-date confirmation or an auditable complete effective rule-and-exception inventory.
3. Original provider publication/revision timestamps are absent, so the 17:45 data-finalization convention is an exploratory assumption; the one-session-lag sensitivity breaks all 120 frozen decisions.

To change this decision, reconcile the ten cells with independent electronic evidence, resolve and hash the nine CME date schedules, and version an accepted exploratory availability/clock policy before rerunning the same 120-date gate. Strict certification additionally needs independent venue completeness and point-in-time receipt evidence. No certification promotion or HAR fit is authorized by this candidate output.
