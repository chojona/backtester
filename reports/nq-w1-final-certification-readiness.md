## 1. Official calendar

[CME's equity-hours notice](https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/electronic-trading/2021/06/20210621.html) establishes ordinary NQ Globex trading Sunday–Friday **17:00–16:00 Central**, daily maintenance **16:00–17:00 Central**, and removal of the former 15-minute equity pause. Its affected-product attachment includes NQ. The ordinary 2022 active interval is 18:00 previous day–17:00 trade date New York time: **22:00–21:00 UTC in EDT** and **23:00–22:00 UTC in EST**, 1,380 minutes. The previous-day evening reopening belongs to the next trade date. The [CME 2022 Globex holiday archive](https://www.cmegroup.com/content/cmegroup/cn-s/tools-information/holiday-calendar.html) lists the relevant Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, and Labor Day schedule workbooks and cautions that hours can change.

Four preserved original `.xls` workbooks, with binary hashes in the [calendar evidence review](nq-w1-final-calendar-readiness.md), positively identify the plain `Equity Products` row, not the TACO exception row. Their Tuesday trade-date headers combine the preceding Sunday/Monday activity into **May 31, June 21, July 5, and September 6**, respectively. Each has two active UTC intervals totaling **2,520 minutes**: Sunday 22:00–Monday 17:00 and Monday 22:00–Tuesday 21:00. The Monday 17:00–22:00 halt/pre-open is not an active trading interval. Memorial is outside the 120-date sample but determines the June 1 causal boundary. The three Monday civil labels inside the sample (June 20, July 4, September 5) are not separate CME trade-date identities. Thus 123 civil weekdays reduce to 120 candidate identities without skipping an unexplained weekday.

Nine dates remain **calendar-provisional**: **June 22, July 6, September 7, October 10–12, and November 9–11**. The first three follow documented holiday Tuesday closes and have full ordinary-rule bars, so the ordinary continuation is supported, but the workbooks do not directly list their Wednesday NQ hours. The October and November dates likewise have full ordinary-rule grids, yet the archive has no dated Globex NQ exception schedule for their Columbus/Veterans guard windows. The available clearing and settlement notices concern processing, not NQ open/close and trade-date boundaries. An archive omission or a full OHLCV grid is **not positive proof that no revised NQ-specific exception existed**. A dated exchange NQ schedule, historical reference-data response, or auditable complete rule-and-exception inventory is needed to clear these nine. Ordinary dates outside these guards are supported by the general notice and observed grids, but this audit does not assert a date-by-date CME exception attestation for all 120.

## 2. Active minutes and no-trade evidence

The candidate grid has **169,020** active minutes and **169,015** distinct selected-contract OHLCV bars. There are no selected duplicate or off-grid bars. Five dates each lack one bar. All ten preserved `GLBX.MDP3` responses were rechecked for exact raw symbol, schema, inclusive/exclusive UTC bounds, `limit=null`, `partial=[]`, `not_found=[]`, local DBN and JSONL hashes, and response counts. The five bounded `trades` responses return **zero records**. The paired session-open-through-gap `status` responses return **2, 3, 3, 3, and 2 records**, respectively; their returned state is `is_trading=Y`, `is_quoting=Y`, with no returned pause or halt through the gap. The [investigation report](nq-w1-five-gap-investigation.md) gives the exact file hashes and adjacent-bar instrument checks; the new JSON includes every response's bound, count, and hash.

`OBSERVED` means an actual retained, published trade bar at the expected minute. `PROVIDER_NO_TRADE` means a hash-bound, exactly scoped zero-trade Databento response plus active status within that provider's returned stream; it is a carried clock-price sample, **not** a new trade, OHLCV bar, or exchange-wide completeness attestation. `UNKNOWN` means the expected active minute has neither acceptable observed bar nor bounded no-trade evidence, or its source completeness cannot be supported at the required standard. Under the experimental provider-stream standard there are **169,015 OBSERVED, five PROVIDER_NO_TRADE, and zero unclassified candidate-grid minutes**. At exchange-completeness and frozen HAR eligibility level the five remain unresolved: a common upstream loss could affect both trade and trade-derived OHLCV feeds, and status is not a venue-sequence completeness proof.

The general [`docs/VOLATILITY.md`](../docs/VOLATILITY.md) rule allows an **explicit provider-evidenced `NO_TRADE` bar** as a separately counted flat minute. The **frozen HAR study is narrower**: it prescribes completed one-minute bars, full expected coverage and matched 1m/5m/15m support, and says not to zero-fill or connect across an unobserved gap. The experimental [`nq-clock-returns-v2.0.0`](nq-clock-returns-v2-validation.md) calculation legitimately carries these five prices for arithmetic, but that calculation does **not** amend the preregistered study or its eligibility gate. To admit the five, issue a **new versioned preregistration**, for example `nq-har-exploratory-study-design-v1.1.0` and a new immutable sample manifest, **before any fitting**. It must explicitly adopt `nq-clock-returns-v2.0.0` return references and segment-anchored RV5, define evidence-backed provider no-trade samples as eligible clock coverage and their required exchange/provider completeness standard, specify 1m/5m/15m matched support and availability rules, and retain the unchanged 120-consecutive-session minimum. Until then these five dates fail the frozen policy. A policy amendment alone would not prove venue completeness.

## 3. Causal contract replay

I independently summed raw Parquet volume by corrected CME trade date and contract, enumerated **contracts present on the current date**, then selected the maximum preceding completed trade-date volume with the saved contract-code tie rule. All **120** winners and saved per-contract ranks reproduced; none uses same-day or future volume. June 1's predecessor is the corrected combined May 31, whose retained NQM22/NQU22/NQZ22 volumes are **821,154 / 1,785 / 39**. June 1 NQM22 volume **672,372** then selects NQM22 for June 2. The combined holiday trade dates were used as single predecessor dates: June 21, July 5, and September 6 volume feeds the following session. The two switches are **June 14 to NQU22** (June 13 retained volume NQU22 **610,767** versus NQM22 **387,941**) and **September 13 to NQZ22** (September 12 NQZ22 **367,205** versus NQU22 **313,361**).

This is a reproducible **retained-panel** decision, not a complete-universe proof. The source table can enumerate only contracts it contains on each current date; it cannot show a missing eligible outright. Thin deferred contracts have sparse predecessor bars, including only **129** NQZ22 bars on June 13 and **59** NQH23 bars on September 12. A missing bar, upstream trade loss, or missing entire contract could change true volume. Even a large observed winning margin is not a formal upper bound on absent volume. Therefore all 120 causal decisions, plus the external June 1 boundary, remain **unverified for eligible-universe and full-prior-volume completeness**; the exact retained winner is still recorded per date in the JSON.

## 4. HAR chronology and availability

The 120 dates yield **22 feature warmup sessions** and **98 potential next-session targets**. Session ordinals 23–72 are **50 train targets (July 6–September 14)**; 73–92 are **20 validation targets (September 15–October 12)**; 93–120 are **28 test targets (October 13–November 21)**. For every potential row, I recomputed the 1-, 5-, and 22-session arithmetic means from the versioned daily RV5 series, matched its target RV5, checked the exact ordered 22 feature dates, and checked `feature close < target open < target close`. All 98 pass arithmetic chronology. The JSON records each feature and target close timestamp and split.

An RV is mathematically complete **after** its last required minute closes, so the previous session's close is the earliest nominal feature origin. The saved bars do not establish the historical Databento publication or revision time. Accordingly the 98 as-of checks prove **structural causality under immediate-close availability**, not that a researcher could have received the final vendor values at those times. The current target is never in its own features. Current-day contract presence is used only for the target day's selected series; target RV and future contract-selection volume are not in earlier feature arithmetic. Live or historical as-of validity needs source publication/revision evidence or a conservative availability convention fixed in the new versioned study.
