Date of review: 2026-10-14 (America/New_York). Baseline: [NQ certification feasibility](nq-certification-feasibility.md). **Decision: strict certification of the 2022-01-05–2022-12-23 candidate is BLOCKED.** This is an evidence audit, not a new certification version. ## Scope and method

The 15 sampled dates span the window boundary, four causal contract switches, ordinary full and sparse sessions, six holiday or adjacent sessions, Thanksgiving, and the unobserved Good Friday candidate. They are 2022-01-05, 01-17, 02-16, 03-15, 04-15, 05-30, 06-14, 06-20, 07-27, 09-13, 10-13, 11-24, 11-25, 12-13, and 12-23. April 15 is an unobserved weekday candidate, not an observed trading session.

I read the retained consolidated quarterly-outright OHLCV Parquet independently of the Phase 3 classifier, selected the contract from the existing session decision file, generated a separate minute grid with `zoneinfo` in America/New_York, and compared timestamp sets. I independently summed the retained preceding session's volume by contract and reapplied the existing causal rule (highest prior-session volume among contracts present today, contract code as tie breaker). This checks reproducibility of the *retained* decision; it does not make an incomplete predecessor complete. The ordinary 2022 candidate grid is **[18:00 ET on the previous calendar date, 17:00 ET on the trade date)**, 1,380 one-minute starts, with no afternoon pause. The 2021 CME notice took effect for trade date 2021-06-28 and explicitly lists NQ among the affected futures. The ordinary grid is source backed as a regime, but individual 2022 exceptions still require dated proof. All intervals below are half-open and use the local EST/EDT designation shown.

## Recoverability and whole-window estimate

Local work can reproduce volume leaders, isolate exact minute holes, reconcile retained file hashes, and rerun a versioned calendar/contract decision **after** independent dates and statuses are documented. Full selected bars on a guarded predecessor may clear a causal propagation failure once its dated CME schedule is checked. No local transform can establish that 2022-04-15 was closed, turn an OHLCV gap into an exchange pause, or prove that a retained volume leader is the true leader when prior eligible-contract delivery is incomplete.

Independent **CME NQ** schedules are needed for all 252 observed trade dates and the intervening Apr 15 weekday, including trade-date assignment and any exceptional pauses/reopenings. The holiday spreadsheets are a promising no-purchase source, but their product rows were inaccessible in this run. Independent provider completeness/trade-status or exchange records are needed for each minute that remains expected after dated CME grids are applied; the current acquisition retained OHLCV and definitions, not those records. The baseline identifies 18 sparse dates and 1,920 ordinary-grid absent minutes across the full window, 41 incomplete causal decisions, 32 guarded observed dates, and one unobserved weekday candidate. This pilot found ordinary and Thanksgiving overnight holes among those 18; therefore calendar work alone cannot finish the window. No free accessible source identified in this review resolves those minute statuses. **Completing strict certification for the entire span with the evidence currently readable and retained is infeasible.** It is conditionally feasible only if the dated CME NQ schedules and independent minute-status/completeness records can be obtained without a paid request, then an isolated recertification confirms every expected session passes all four unchanged gates. Until then strict HAR remains NO-GO.

## Follow-up evidence review — 2026-10-14

The archived `.xls` links were revisited as spreadsheet sources. No workbook bytes could be obtained for a reader: the web fetcher rejects the legacy Excel MIME type, shell DNS resolution for `www.cmegroup.com` failed, and the in-app browser blocked the direct file URL. Chrome computer access was not granted. No local `.xls` or `.xlsx` copy was found in the workspace or `/tmp`. Installing or selecting an Excel parser cannot reveal the NQ rows without the source file. The CME holiday index remains a pointer to evidence, not the evidence itself; precise holiday intervals and document revision/effective timestamps remain unresolved.

[Databento's historical condition API](https://databento.com/docs/api-reference-historical) defines `available` as no **known** dataset issue and `degraded` as possibly missing or incorrect data. It reports by UTC date and includes a last-modified date, not a per-contract, per-minute completeness certificate. [Databento's OHLCV specification](https://databento.com/docs/knowledge-base) says no bar is printed when there is no trade, so an absent OHLCV minute cannot alone distinguish no trade, missing delivery, or exchange closure. [Status records](https://databento.com/docs/schemas-and-data-formats/status) can identify trading, halt, pause, and close states; Databento also documents an [NQ Globex status example](https://databento.com/docs/examples/basics-historical/halts). These are **potential independent adjudication records**, not records retained in this acquisition. A status stream alone would establish an exchange pause where present but would not prove that no trade occurred during every otherwise-open minute; trade/sequence or provider completeness evidence would still be needed where a bar is absent. No billed timeseries request was made.

The minimum evidence packet for a contiguous first NQ HAR window is: (1) a dated CME NQ open/pause/close and trade-date grid for every candidate date and boundary predecessor, including proof of the Apr 15 status; (2) independent status and trade/completeness evidence for each still-expected missing minute, with provider revision and source integrity checks; (3) complete prior-session volumes for all eligible contracts and every causal switch; (4) an isolated, versioned recertification that leaves the existing strict gates and source artifacts unchanged. The research-design floor remains **778 consecutive accepted sessions**: 22 lag warm-up observations, 504 initial training responses, and 252 ordered out-of-sample responses that can be allocated to validation and test. The 2022 candidate contains only 252 observed dates even if all its evidence cleared, so it cannot alone satisfy that floor. Current strict history has only 11 Tier A sessions, split into segments of 10 and 1. **Historical HAR fitting remains NO-GO.**
